SEO Services in Lahore: What I Actually Do

Good SEO is several jobs working together. After reviewing your site I’ll recommend the ones that matter for your situation instead of selling everything on this list.

Website audit and technical SEO

Google can only rank pages it can crawl, render and index. I check indexing in Search Console, page speed and Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, duplicate pages, redirects, broken links and structured data. You get a prioritised fix list your developer can act on, not a dump of tool output.

Keyword research for the Lahore market

I map the phrases your customers actually use to specific pages, based on search intent. People in Lahore search in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, and a search like “AC repair Lahore” needs a different page from “how to clean an AC filter”. Getting that match right is usually where the biggest early gains are.

On-page SEO and content

Titles, headings, internal links, service pages and articles, written to answer the questions buyers ask before they call. Good content includes details only you can supply: how your process works, what affects your pricing, what you know about your local customers. I’ll draw those out of you rather than invent them.

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

If customers find you by area, whether that’s Gulberg, DHA, Johar Town, Model Town or Bahria Town, your Google Business Profile, reviews, consistent business details and location pages matter as much as your website. I’ll review or set up your profile, fix inconsistencies and advise on earning genuine reviews. The profile is tied to your premises and your customers, so you stay the owner and I work with the access you give me.

Link building and digital PR

Quality over quantity: relevant citations, partnerships and genuine mentions from sites your customers actually read. No paid link networks, no automated spam, nothing that puts your site at risk of a penalty.

Reporting you can read

Each month you get a short report: organic traffic, clicks and impressions from Search Console, movement on agreed keywords, enquiries from organic search where tracking allows, and the priorities for the next month. If something didn’t work, the report says so.

Independent SEO Consultant or Agency: Which Suits You?

Both can work well. It depends on what you need.

Working with me suits you if you want the person doing the audit to be the person on the call, your needs are focused on one site, a handful of key services or a local market, and you want a straightforward conversation about priorities and budget.

An agency may suit you better if you need a large content team, paid ads, design and development under one roof, or you are managing many sites or locations at once.

If your needs lean towards the second group, I’ll say so on our first call and tell you what to look for.

How to Choose an SEO Consultant in Lahore

The market in Lahore is crowded, from freelancers and agencies to marketplace profiles, and it’s hard to judge if SEO isn’t your field. Whoever you hire, ask:

  • Can you show real work for a business like mine, and what exactly did you do?
  • Who will do the work: you, a junior team, or an outsourced provider?
  • What access do you need, and why?
  • How do you build links, and could any of it put my site at risk?
  • How will you report, and what can I check myself?
  • Do you promise rankings? (If the answer is yes, walk away.)

Google’s own guide, Do you need an SEO?, is a useful checklist too. I’m happy to answer every question on this list before you commit to anything.

SEO Questions from Lahore Businesses

How much do SEO services in Lahore cost?

It depends on your competition, the size of your site, how much needs fixing and whether you also need content and link building. I quote after reviewing your site so the price matches the actual work. Whatever we agree, the scope and deliverables are written down. Be cautious of very low prices: real SEO takes skilled time, and cheap packages often rely on templates or automated links.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Usually months, not days. Technical and indexing fixes can show up within weeks, while competitive keywords take sustained work. Local searches can move sooner when your Google Business Profile and website are both in good shape. I’ll give you a realistic view for your keywords after the audit.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings on Google?

No. Rankings are decided by Google, not by me or any other SEO. I commit to the work, the reporting and the honesty of the advice, and you can verify progress in your own Search Console account.

What’s the difference between an SEO expert and an SEO consultant?

The titles overlap. A consultant tends to focus on advice: audits, strategy and priorities. An expert tends to be hands-on. I do both: I plan the work and, where it’s needed, I do it or brief your developer to do it.

Will SEO help my business show on Google Maps?

It can, if you have a physical location or a defined service area. Maps visibility relies on your Google Business Profile, reviews, consistent business details and the relevance of your website. I’ll look at all four during the audit.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your website address, your main goal (calls, online orders, shop visits), your best-selling services, any competitors you already watch, and read-only access to Search Console and Analytics if you have them. If you’ve had SEO work done before, tell me what was done.
